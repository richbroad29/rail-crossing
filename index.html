Know when the barriers will come down

railcrossing.uk predicts when the barriers at a level crossing will close, and when they'll open again, for every train: passenger, freight and empty trains. It's free, works on any phone, and you can add it to your home screen like an app.

What is railcrossing.uk?

A free website that predicts when level crossing barriers will be down, so you can avoid waiting or plan a different route.

How does it know when the barriers will close?

It combines the train timetable, live running times from National Rail, and Network Rail's signalling data, which shows where each train is on the line. Predictions firm up as a train gets closer: from the timetable hours ahead, live times in the last two hours, then the signalling in the final few minutes.

Does it include freight trains?

Yes. Freight and empty trains aren't on station departure boards, but they still close the barriers, so they come from the timetable and are confirmed by the signalling when they're seen. Some freight trains only run when needed; those are marked "may not run" until one is seen.

How accurate is it?

The times are estimates: least certain hours ahead, most accurate in the last few minutes. If a train is held at a signal the barriers can stay down longer, and the app shows "held" rather than guessing.

Which crossings are covered?

One so far: Portslade Level Crossing, on Boundary Road in Brighton and Hove (East Sussex). Every other road level crossing in Great Britain, 1,415 more, is on the map as coming soon.

Can I request my crossing?

Yes. Tap its grey dot on the map, or request a crossing here. Crossings with the most requests, and those where the railway publishes barrier data, are the quickest to add.

Is this an official Network Rail service?

No. railcrossing.uk is independent. It uses open data from Network Rail and National Rail, but neither runs or checks it.

Is it safe to rely on?

No. It's for planning your journey, not for safety: always obey the lights and barriers at the crossing.

Is it free?

Yes: no ads and no sign-up. If you want to contribute to the development of the project, you can contribute via our GoFundMe here.

Can I add it as an app?

Not yet, but you can do something just as good by adding it to your home screen: tap "Add as app" at the top of the map, or on iPhone use Share → Add to Home Screen.
