    Connecting to the train service

    How to read this map

    Every shape is a train. It points the way it is travelling, and its colour says what kind of train it is. Tap a train to see where it is going and what we know about it.

      Estimated positions

      Trains drawn with a dashed outline and a ~tilde after the code are estimated. The signalling system only tells us which short section of track a train is in, so between stations we place it along the line. A faint circle round a train means we only know roughly where it is.

      Where the data comes from

      Train positions come from Network Rail's open signalling feed and timetable. They are updated every few seconds, but they are not an official service. Do not use this map to decide whether you can cross a railway line.
