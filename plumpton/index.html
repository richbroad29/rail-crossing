About this crossing

Where
Plumpton Lane, Plumpton Green, Lewes, East Sussex
Nearest station
Plumpton, under 0.1 miles in a straight line
Type
Barriers lowered by the signaller, with obstacle detection checking the crossing is clear (Network Rail type MCB-OD)
What happens
A signaller lowers full barriers across the whole road, and radar or laser obstacle detection checks it is clear before the train is allowed through, so closures last longer than at an automatic crossing.
