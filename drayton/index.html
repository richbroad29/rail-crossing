About this crossing

Where
Drayton Lane, Oving, Chichester, West Sussex
Nearest station
Chichester, 2.0 miles in a straight line
Type
Barriers lowered by a signaller watching the crossing on CCTV (Network Rail type MCB-CCTV)
What happens
A signaller watches the crossing on CCTV, lowers full barriers across the whole road and checks it is clear before the train is allowed through. The barriers come down well before the train arrives, so closures last longer than at an automatic crossing. At Portslade, a crossing of the same type, half of all closures last between 2 and 3¾ minutes (recorded by this app, November 2026).
